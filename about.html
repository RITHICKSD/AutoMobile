SYSTEM QUERIES

Decoded answers to common operational questions.

Does tuning void my warranty?

Autocore offers both hard-flashes and piggyback systems. Our piggyback ECUs can be removed flawlessly before dealer servicing, maintaining your original warranty intact.

How long does a Vortex Scan take?

A standard telemetry pull takes 45 minutes. A deep structural analysis involving dyno pulls and thermal imaging takes up to 4 hours.

Do you tune electric vehicles (EVs)?

Yes. We rewrite power delivery algorithms for dual-motor systems to favor raw acceleration or sustained endurance, depending on driver preference.
